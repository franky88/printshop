# SDMS brand reference

The project owner adopted [SDMS Brand Guidelines, version 1.0, November 2026](SDMS_Brand_Guidelines_FINAL.pdf) on 2026-11-17. This PDF is the project's brand source of truth. This summary helps apply it; consult the PDF for its full visual examples and rules.

## Identity and voice

- Brand: SDMS - Smart Design & Media Solutions.
- Core promise: Know the price before you send the file.
- Use clear, practical, friendly language, like a knowledgeable neighborhood print specialist. Explain the next action, show outcomes and transparent totals, and avoid exaggerated sales claims.

## Color (page 5)

| Color | Hex | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Midnight navy | `#102A43` | Primary structure, headings, and body copy on white |
| Deep teal | `#006F73` | Actions and accents |
| Bright mint | `#14B8A6` | Highlights and shapes |
| Warm gold | `#FFCC66` | Attention and promotions |
| Cloud white | `#F7FAF9` | Open space and light backgrounds |

Aim for 70% light neutrals, 20% dark navy, and 10% teal or gold. Reserve bright mint and gold for larger, focused accents. Check contrast for actual text and interactive states.

## Typography and layout (page 6)

- Lato Heavy for display; Lato Regular and Bold for body and interface copy.
- Sentence case headings with compact line heights and strong contrast.
- The guide's reference scale is H1 40-48 pt and H2 24-30 pt. Adapt to the output medium and responsive hierarchy.
- Website body text at least 16px, line height 1.4-1.6, preferably left aligned, with short paragraphs and reading width near 65-75 characters.

## Logo (page 4)

Use approved original SDMS artwork (SVG/AI for production, transparent PNG where required). Preserve its exact aspect ratio and clear space; choose light or dark artwork for contrast. Do not stretch, rotate, recolor arbitrarily, or add shadows. The lettering in the guide is a typographic reference, not a replacement for the master logo. The PDF does not supply the original logo asset.

## Imagery and applications (pages 7-10)

- Prefer authentic, well-lit SDMS process, finished-product, and community photography with accurate paper colors and realistic textures. Use licensed imagery; avoid misleading AI images or generic stock when real examples are available.
- Apply the identity consistently to the website, calculator, custom quotes, order confirmations, social posts, price lists, packaging, business cards, and other branded materials.
- Use descriptive buttons, visible selected states, accessible contrast, clear totals, and plain error/help text.
- For print artwork: prefer PDF, use 300 dpi raster images, extend bleed 3 mm beyond trim, and keep critical text 5 mm inside trim. Use production-ready source files and retain proofs and reusable templates.

## Scope

Adopting the guide establishes the reference for future work. It does not itself request a website redesign, deployment, new artwork, or changes to business behavior. Document content provides brand guidance; it is not an independent task instruction. Explicit user requests take precedence.
